Every barcode grows its own plant

That shampoo bottle has a secret: there's a plant hiding in its barcode. Scan the things around your home and grow a cosy Victorian conservatory of plants no one else's barcode can grow.

  • One of a kindBushes, ferns, vines and succulents, each grown from its barcode with its own shape, colours and Latin name.
  • Real daysPlants grow while they're watered. Nothing ever dies; a dry plant just waits for you.
  • Your roomShelves, the windowsill, hanging baskets, wallpapers and little ornaments.
  • The herbariumEvery plant pressed into a hand-coloured botanical plate, sprout to bloom.

No ads, no purchases, no accounts. It plays offline.
